# Credits

What this repository and the film use that the team did not make. The code's own dependencies are in each
`package.json` and in `vision/requirements.txt`.

## Data

- Map data © [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) contributors, ODbL 1.0.
- Passenger statistics for Craiova Airport: Eurostat.
- Airport layout: the Romanian AIP (ROMATSA), OpenStreetMap, and our own photos and video of Terminal 2.

## Software and models

- [Ultralytics YOLO11](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics) (AGPL-3.0) and ByteTrack, for people detection and tracking (`vision/`, `--source yolo`).
- [LightGBM](https://github.com/microsoft/LightGBM) (MIT), NumPy and SciPy (BSD), OpenCV (Apache-2.0).
- React, Vite, Playwright, Vitest, Cloudflare Workers tooling and the other libraries in each `package.json`.
- The WhatsApp agent calls Anthropic's Claude API when a key is configured.
- The WhatsApp and login layers were ported from Moni, Bogdan's earlier project.

## The film

- Music and sound effects: made in code for the film, the music by our own script and the effects with the [earshot](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/earshot) sound bank by Leon Lin.
- Voice: a synthetic Romanian voice (ElevenLabs).
- Type: Outfit and Geist (SIL Open Font License).
- The digital twin of Terminal 2 seen in the film is a private Unity project (Unity 6.3, HDRP). Its exterior shots use a
  ground texture derived from satellite imagery, Imagery © Google; that imagery is not part of this repository.
  People: Microsoft Rocketbox avatars (MIT). Textures: ambientCG (CC0). Terrain: Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (© DLR e.V.,
  © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA). Land cover: ESA WorldCover
  (CC BY 4.0). Trees: Meta and WRI High Resolution Canopy Height Maps (CC BY 4.0). Building footprints: Microsoft ML
  building footprints (CDLA-Permissive-2.0) via Overture Maps.
